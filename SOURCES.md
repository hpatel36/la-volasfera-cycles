# Sources

## La Volasfera symbolic degrees

The default symbolic images and interpretations were transcribed from:

Charubel and Sepharial, *The Degrees of the Zodiac Symbolized*, 1902.

Digital scan:
<https://iapsop.com/ssoc/1902__charubel___degrees_of_the_zodiac_symbolized.pdf>

The La Volasfera text is attributed in that edition to Sepharial (Walter Gorn
Old, 1864–1929). The source edition and its text are in the public domain. The
JSON file in `data/la_volasfera_degree_definitions.json` is a transcription for
structured lookup; it does not contain Sabian symbol material or modern
third-party commentary.
